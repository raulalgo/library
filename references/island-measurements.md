# Island measurements

Source: Daniel Bruce's construction drawing `island-rev-B-for-construction.pdf` (rev B, 6 Sep 2024), plus the email thread. Millimetres, as drawn. Numbers follow the list in `PLAN.md`. The drawing is the design, not a survey of the built island.

Orientation: the lounge side (stools) has the knee well and the oak bookshelf. The kitchen side has six drawers and two cupboard doors. Seen from the lounge side, the knee well is on the left and the bookshelf on the right.

## Island

1. Overall length: 2000 (worktop). Leg frame: 1990.
2. Overall depth: 750 (worktop). Leg frame on the drawer end: 740.
3. Floor to top of worktop: 918.
4. Worktop: 12, Hi-Macs Urban Concrete, matte.
5. Knee well: 977.5 wide, 230 deep (worktop front edge to the blue lining panel). Short ends: the worktop sits 5 past the leg frame.
6. Corner radius: R80, one corner only (lounge side, knee-well end).
7. Blue section on the lounge side (knee well): 977.5. Bookshelf section: 1017.5 (45 + 927.5 + 45).
8. Blue end panels: 22.
9. Floor to underside of carcass (top of frame rail): 245. Floor to underside of frame rail: 200. Rail height: 45.
10. Legs: 45 × 45, solid oak with whitened oil finish. Leg spacing on the kitchen side: 45 | 1136.5 | 45 | 718.5 | 45. The knee-well end has an extra leg 225 in from the lounge edge.

Carcass height: 661 (245 + 661 + 12 = 918).

## Shelf section

11. Inner width: 927.5, between 45 uprights.
12. Inner depth: 271.
13. Bottom shelf top surface: ~267 from the floor (19 board).
14. Middle shelf top surface: ~586.5 from the floor. Thickness: 19.
15. Clear height above the middle shelf: 300.5, up to a 19 top board under the worktop. Lower opening: also 300.5.
16. Shelves open to the lounge/stool side. Oak veneer, white-tint oil.

## Kitchen side

- Drawers: two columns of three, each column 587 wide. Front heights from top: 194, 227.5, 227.5, with 3 gaps.
- Cupboards: two 378-wide doors. Inside: a narrow full-height cupboard for baking trays (200) and a wider one (510) with an adjustable shelf.
- Colour: Little Greene Mazarine 256, PU spray, 25% sheen.

## Stools

17. Seat height: 680 (Raúl). Still to measure: seat length × width, leg thickness, footrest height.
